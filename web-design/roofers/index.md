## A few practical questions.

What will my website cost?

We agree the scope and quote first. The number of pages, content, design and integrations shape the project. Hosting, ongoing marketing and automation are priced separately, so you know what the build includes.

Can you work with my existing website?

Yes. We review the current pages, useful content, search traffic and enquiry routes before deciding what to retain or rebuild. Existing URLs and redirects are part of the launch plan.

Can you turn our project photos into useful pages?

Yes. We pair the images with the roof type, scope, location and process, then create a consistent format for future projects.

Can the site focus on replacements or larger projects?

Yes. We can prioritise your preferred work through the headline, services, case examples and estimate form.

Can we keep our current domain?

Yes. We can build around your existing domain and map current page addresses to the new structure during a redesign.

Can you help follow up on estimates?

Yes. Enquiry routing, reminders and quote follow-up can be scoped around the tools your business uses.
