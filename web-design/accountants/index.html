A few practical
questions.

What will my website cost?

We agree the scope and quote first. The number of pages, content, design and integrations shape the project. Hosting, ongoing marketing and automation are priced separately, so you know what the build includes.

Can you work with my existing website?

Yes. We review the current pages, useful content, search traffic and enquiry routes before deciding what to retain or rebuild. Existing URLs and redirects are part of the launch plan.

Can we focus on recurring business clients?

Yes. We can prioritise ongoing services, relevant industries and the value of the working relationship throughout the site.

Can clients still use our current portal?

Yes. Existing clients can reach your portal directly while prospective clients follow a separate enquiry route.

Can we change our new-client availability?

Yes. We can make that message straightforward to update as your team’s capacity changes.

Can you write the service pages?

Yes. I draft them from your actual offer and experience, then work through your team’s review.
