## A few practical questions.

What will my website cost?

We agree the scope and quote first. The number of pages, content, design and integrations shape the project. Hosting, ongoing marketing and automation are priced separately, so you know what the build includes.

Can you work with my existing website?

Yes. We review the current pages, useful content, search traffic and enquiry routes before deciding what to retain or rebuild. Existing URLs and redirects are part of the launch plan.

Can we keep our reservation system?

Yes. We connect the website to the system your team already uses.

Can we update the menu ourselves?

Yes. The menu can be built around a manageable editing process for dishes, prices and seasonal changes.

Can you add private dining or catering enquiries?

Yes. Those services can have dedicated pages and forms that collect useful event details.

Can you help with food photography and social content?

Yes. Original media and campaign assets can be planned alongside the website.
