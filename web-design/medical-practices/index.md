## Make getting to the clinic straightforward.

An Okanagan clinic page should make the actual visit easier: the entrance, parking, accessibility, appointment arrangements and current contact information. If you have more than one location, each needs accurate details and a clear connection to the services offered there.

## Useful information, without the search.

I structure the site around patient tasks and the clinic’s current operation. Someone looking for appointment information should not need to read an introduction to the practice before finding the answer.

Service descriptions and clinician profiles use wording reviewed by your team. The design gives them a clear hierarchy and enough space to read comfortably on a phone.

The public website stays focused on information and access. Appointment systems, records and clinical communication continue through the established tools your practice uses. We make the links between them easy to understand.

## Built to be found. Ready to be chosen.

Accurate location details, clinician-approved service information and consistent business listings support Google and AI search. We establish who maintains changes so the public information stays useful.

I handle the page hierarchy, H1s and H2s, titles, descriptions, internal links, structured data and sitemap. Your services, location and business information stay consistent across the website. We can add Google Business Profile management and useful local pages as the business grows.

“How do I book an appointment at this Kelowna clinic?”A useful page explains the clinic’s current access and booking process, then directs the patient to the appropriate tool or contact route. I make that information readable and easy to find.

## A few practical questions.

What will my website cost?

We agree the scope and quote first. The number of pages, content, design and integrations shape the project. Hosting, ongoing marketing and automation are priced separately, so you know what the build includes.

Can you work with my existing website?

Yes. We review the current pages, useful content, search traffic and enquiry routes before deciding what to retain or rebuild. Existing URLs and redirects are part of the launch plan.

Can we keep our booking system?

Yes. We connect the website to the appointment tools your practice already uses.

Can reception update hours and notices?

Yes. We can provide straightforward editing for the information that changes most often.

Is this only useful for attracting new patients?

The site can also help existing patients and reduce repeated administrative questions. The priorities follow your practice’s needs.

Who reviews the medical service descriptions?

Your clinical team reviews and approves those descriptions before publication.
