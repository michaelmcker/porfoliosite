A few practical
questions.

What will my website cost?

We agree the scope and quote first. The number of pages, content, design and integrations shape the project. Hosting, ongoing marketing and automation are priced separately, so you know what the build includes.

Can you work with my existing website?

Yes. We review the current pages, useful content, search traffic and enquiry routes before deciding what to retain or rebuild. Existing URLs and redirects are part of the launch plan.

Can we give EV charging its own page?

Yes. We can explain residential or strata work, your assessment process, relevant examples and the enquiry route in a dedicated page.

Can I add new projects myself?

Yes. A reusable project layout and a short handover make it easier to add photos, scope and location without redesigning the site.

Will the website work with our existing quote process?

We start by reviewing that process. Forms, email routing and existing tools can be connected around the information your team needs.

Can you create ads or project videos too?

Yes. Website content, project films, social assets and service-specific advertising can be planned together and quoted as the work you need.
