## Make the visit easy to plan.

For an Okanagan clinic, location information should include the actual entrance, parking, accessibility and current hours. Multiple clinics need separate location pages with the right team and services. The details help people choose an appointment they can actually attend.

## Let the website do the explaining.

I build the content around your clinic’s services and the questions that come up before a first appointment. Clear service pages help a visitor find the right route without asking them to navigate a catalogue of unfamiliar terms.

Your existing booking platform should feel like a natural next step. We connect relevant pages to the appropriate service or practitioner where the platform supports it, and explain any choice the patient needs to make.

The design brings team photography, clinic imagery and readable information together. Your staff can keep fees, biographies and practical details current through a consistent editing process.

## Built to be found. Ready to be chosen.

Google Business Profile consistency, clear service pages and useful FAQs support search discovery. The same factual content gives AI search a clear description of your clinic and how to contact it.

I handle the page hierarchy, H1s and H2s, titles, descriptions, internal links, structured data and sitemap. Your services, location and business information stay consistent across the website. We can add Google Business Profile management and useful local pages as the business grows.

“Where can I book a physiotherapy appointment in Kelowna?”Present the clinic’s services, practitioner information, first-visit details and a direct booking route. I connect those answers with the technical structure that makes the page easy to read and discover.

## A few practical questions.

What will my website cost?

We agree the scope and quote first. The number of pages, content, design and integrations shape the project. Hosting, ongoing marketing and automation are priced separately, so you know what the build includes.

Can you work with my existing website?

Yes. We review the current pages, useful content, search traffic and enquiry routes before deciding what to retain or rebuild. Existing URLs and redirects are part of the launch plan.

Can you work with our current booking software?

Yes. We design the website around the booking tools your clinic already uses.

Can each practitioner have a page?

Yes. A consistent biography format can show their approach, services, location and booking link.

Can we publish fees and billing information?

Yes. We can make your current information easy to find and update.

Can you support more than one clinic location?

Yes. Each location can show the correct hours, practitioners, services and appointment route.
