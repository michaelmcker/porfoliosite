## Help Okanagan families plan a real visit.

Families considering your school need to understand its location, travel arrangements and relationship with the surrounding community. We use actual school information and local photography to explain those details, with an easy way to arrange a conversation or tour.

## Show the school that families will actually experience.

Your school has a distinctive culture, setting and educational approach. I help organise that story around the decisions families make, using real photography and clear explanations of the school day, programs and community.

Admissions information should be practical: grades offered, the application process, published fees, visit arrangements and who can answer a question. Those details can sit comfortably within a beautiful, welcoming design.

A consistent content system makes it easier for staff to update dates, notices and program information. The public site can connect to the parent tools you already use without duplicating all of their functions.

## Built to be found. Ready to be chosen.

Clear admissions, program and location pages help Google and AI search understand the school. Current content and consistent organisation information make the site useful to families finding you for the first time.

I handle the page hierarchy, H1s and H2s, titles, descriptions, internal links, structured data and sitemap. Your services, location and business information stay consistent across the website. We can add Google Business Profile management and useful local pages as the business grows.

“How do I arrange a visit to an independent school in Kelowna?”The admissions page should explain who to contact, what the visit involves and how it fits into the application process. I connect that information to grades, school life and practical family questions.

## A few practical questions.

What will my website cost?

We agree the scope and quote first. The number of pages, content, design and integrations shape the project. Hosting, ongoing marketing and automation are priced separately, so you know what the build includes.

Can you work with my existing website?

Yes. We review the current pages, useful content, search traffic and enquiry routes before deciding what to retain or rebuild. Existing URLs and redirects are part of the launch plan.

Can the site serve current and prospective families?

Yes. We give admissions and daily family tasks clear routes within the same website.

Can staff update calendars and notices?

Yes. We choose an editing system around the people responsible for those updates.

Can you help with fundraising content?

Yes. Donor storytelling, films and campaign pages can be planned alongside the school website.

Can we keep our parent portal?

Yes. The website can direct families to the tools the school already uses.
