## Explain the properties and areas you manage.

Coverage should be useful to an owner: the communities, property types and service arrangements you support across the Okanagan. We can build local pages around a real operation or portfolio, with photographs and practical information that help an owner assess fit.

## Make a complex service feel well organised.

An owner wants to understand who looks after the property and what the relationship includes. I structure the content around your service scope, communication and onboarding rather than a generic list of benefits.

Long-term rental management and short-term accommodation involve different offers. If you provide both, they need distinct content and enquiry routes. Each page should set expectations that match your actual operation.

The public website can work alongside your management platform. Current listings, applications and tenant requests should move into the systems your team uses, with a clear handoff for the visitor.

## Built to be found. Ready to be chosen.

Consistent business information, specific services and clear owner FAQs give Google and AI search a better account of your business. Ongoing content can develop around the questions that reach your team.

I handle the page hierarchy, H1s and H2s, titles, descriptions, internal links, structured data and sitemap. Your services, location and business information stay consistent across the website. We can add Google Business Profile management and useful local pages as the business grows.

“Who manages long-term rental properties in Kelowna?”The page should explain your management offer, property types, area coverage and how an owner can enquire. I organise those facts into service pages with a connected local search structure.

## A few practical questions.

What will my website cost?

We agree the scope and quote first. The number of pages, content, design and integrations shape the project. Hosting, ongoing marketing and automation are priced separately, so you know what the build includes.

Can you work with my existing website?

Yes. We review the current pages, useful content, search traffic and enquiry routes before deciding what to retain or rebuild. Existing URLs and redirects are part of the launch plan.

Can we keep our property-management software?

Yes. We plan the website around your current portals, listing tools and application process.

Can owner and tenant enquiries be separated?

Yes. The navigation and contact routes can direct each audience to the appropriate team or system.

Can you show our service areas?

Yes. We present actual coverage with the property types and services available in each area.

Can we add new properties ourselves?

Yes. We agree the source of listing information and provide an editing process that fits your team.
