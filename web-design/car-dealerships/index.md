Buyers across the Okanagan need to know where the dealership is, when the team is available and how a vehicle enquiry is handled. Accurate location details and a clear confirmation process help people plan a visit from Kelowna or another community in the valley.

The dealership’s identity matters alongside the stock. Your team, location and approach to the buying process give visitors a reason to contact you rather than keep opening listing tabs.

What will my website cost?

We agree the scope and quote first. The number of pages, content, design and integrations shape the project. Hosting, ongoing marketing and automation are priced separately, so you know what the build includes.

Can you work with my existing website?

Yes. We review the current pages, useful content, search traffic and enquiry routes before deciding what to retain or rebuild. Existing URLs and redirects are part of the launch plan.

Can you connect our inventory feed?

We review the feed and platform options first, then scope the inventory integration around the data and access available.

Can enquiries go to our CRM?

Yes. We can connect the enquiry route and preserve the vehicle reference and buyer context.

Can we start with a brand website?

Yes. A focused dealership website can work alongside your existing inventory platform and grow from there.
