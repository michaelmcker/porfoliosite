Service areas with something useful to say.

Kelowna, West Kelowna and Lake Country are different service commitments. We map where you take urgent calls, where you quote planned work and whether commercial coverage is different. Local pages can show a relevant job, the work available there and the next step. That gives a customer useful information before they get in touch.

Make it easy to tell whether you can help.

A service page should answer four questions quickly: do you handle this problem, do you work in this area, what makes you a good choice, and what happens next? I write and design those answers together, so the visitor can make a decision without hunting through menus.

For a water-heater page, that means the types of work you offer, the assessment process, relevant project photos and an obvious quote or call action. For drain work, the copy and contact route can be different. The navigation should reflect the business you actually run.

Availability belongs next to the action. If the office answers during business hours, the page should explain the callback process outside those hours. Clear expectations help your team start better conversations.

A few practical
questions.

What will my website cost?

We agree the scope and quote first. The number of pages, content, design and integrations shape the project. Hosting, ongoing marketing and automation are priced separately, so you know what the build includes.

Can you work with my existing website?

Yes. We review the current pages, useful content, search traffic and enquiry routes before deciding what to retain or rebuild. Existing URLs and redirects are part of the launch plan.

Can the site focus on the plumbing jobs I want more of?

Yes. We prioritise those services in the navigation, content, project examples and enquiry form. Emergency repairs, installations and commercial work can each have a clear route.

Do I need a page for every community?

We start with your real coverage. A separate page earns its place when it can explain local services, show relevant work or answer a different customer question.

Can you connect the site to my booking tools?

Yes. We review the tools you use and connect the right next step, whether that is a callback request, a quote or a confirmed appointment through your scheduling system.

Can you help with Google Business Profile and follow-up?

Yes. Profile support, local search content, campaign pages and enquiry follow-up can be scoped alongside the website, with clear ownership and ongoing costs.
