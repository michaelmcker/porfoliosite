## Make it easy to tell whether you can help.

A service page should answer four questions quickly: do you handle this problem, do you work in this area, what makes you a good choice, and what happens next? I write and design those answers together, so the visitor can make a decision without hunting through menus.

For a water-heater page, that means the types of work you offer, the assessment process, relevant project photos and an obvious quote or call action. For drain work, the copy and contact route can be different. The navigation should reflect the business you actually run.

Availability belongs next to the action. If the office answers during business hours, the page should explain the callback process outside those hours. Clear expectations help your team start better conversations.
