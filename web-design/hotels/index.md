## Show your particular part of the Okanagan.

The value of local content is helping a guest plan the stay. Show the property’s actual setting, nearby experiences, access and travel information. A lakeside hotel in Kelowna and a retreat elsewhere in the valley should tell different stories, grounded in the experience guests can book.

## A direct booking journey with fewer unanswered questions.

A hotel website has to work for people comparing properties and people ready to book. I plan the pages around both: a strong introduction to the stay, followed by room details, amenities, practical policies and availability.

The booking engine is an important part of the scope. We review how it works on mobile, how room pages connect to it and which information the guest carries through. That keeps the website and booking process feeling connected.

Seasonal packages and offers need a format your team can update. We can also create pages for weddings, meetings or groups where those services are part of the property’s business.

## Built to be found. Ready to be chosen.

Room and experience pages, consistent property information and useful FAQs support Google and AI search discovery. We can connect seasonal campaigns to relevant landing pages and measure the path toward the booking engine.

I handle the page hierarchy, H1s and H2s, titles, descriptions, internal links, structured data and sitemap. Your services, location and business information stay consistent across the website. We can add Google Business Profile management and useful local pages as the business grows.

“Where can I book a hotel with lake access in Kelowna?”The site should clearly describe the property’s real amenities, room options, location and booking route. I give those facts a readable structure supported by original imagery.

## Keep the route to a reservation clear.

Guests can move from a room or offer into availability with the relevant context. Group, event and accessibility questions can reach the right team separately. Measurement can follow booking-engine handoffs and completed reservations where the platform supports it.

## A few practical questions.

What will my website cost?

We agree the scope and quote first. The number of pages, content, design and integrations shape the project. Hosting, ongoing marketing and automation are priced separately, so you know what the build includes.

Can you work with my existing website?

Yes. We review the current pages, useful content, search traffic and enquiry routes before deciding what to retain or rebuild. Existing URLs and redirects are part of the launch plan.

Can you use our current booking engine?

Yes. We review its integration options and design the website around the guest’s journey into that system.

Can the site support packages and seasonal offers?

Yes. We can create an editable offer format with the right availability or enquiry action.

Can you show different room types clearly?

Yes. Consistent room pages can compare occupancy, amenities, photographs and booking options.

Can we track direct booking activity?

We can measure website actions and connect booking results where your booking platform provides the necessary access.
