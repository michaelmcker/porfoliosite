## Show the difference you make in the Okanagan.

Local relevance comes from your actual programs, communities and partnerships. A Kelowna program page can explain where it operates, who can access it and how people can support it. Community stories and current information give local supporters a concrete connection to your work.

## Make the mission visible through the work.

A compelling charity website connects the purpose of the organisation to specific programs and people. I help shape the content around what you do, who it serves and how a supporter can contribute.

Original photography, film and carefully chosen stories give the mission a human context. We work from the material your organisation has permission to publish and create a consistent format for future updates.

The donation experience is part of the design. We can use your existing giving platform and make the handoff clear, while program pages, volunteering and contact information remain straightforward to navigate.

## Built to be found. Ready to be chosen.

Readable program pages, consistent organisation details and current FAQs help Google and AI search understand your work. The site can grow around genuine community questions and the information your team already shares.

I handle the page hierarchy, H1s and H2s, titles, descriptions, internal links, structured data and sitemap. Your services, location and business information stay consistent across the website. We can add Google Business Profile management and useful local pages as the business grows.

## A few practical questions.

What will my website cost?

We agree the scope and quote first. The number of pages, content, design and integrations shape the project. Hosting, ongoing marketing and automation are priced separately, so you know what the build includes.

Can you work with my existing website?

Yes. We review the current pages, useful content, search traffic and enquiry routes before deciding what to retain or rebuild. Existing URLs and redirects are part of the launch plan.

Can we keep our donation platform?

Yes. We can design the giving journey around the platform your organisation already uses.

Can the website support recurring giving?

Yes. We can make the recurring option clear and connect it to the functions available in your giving platform.

Can a small team manage the site?

We choose an editing setup around your capacity and provide guidance for the updates you make most often.

Can you create a donor film too?

Yes. Film, campaign messaging and the website can be developed as one connected project.
