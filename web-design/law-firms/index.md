## A considered website for a considered decision.

I organise the site around the questions prospective clients bring to your firm. Practice pages explain the type of work you undertake, the initial process and the relevant team. A family matter and a business transaction should not lead to the same generic paragraph.

Navigation needs to serve people at different stages. Someone referred to a particular lawyer should find that biography quickly. Someone finding you through search should be able to identify the relevant practice and contact route just as easily.

Your team reviews the wording before launch. We establish a repeatable format for biographies, practice updates and articles, so the site can develop with your firm without becoming difficult to maintain.

## A few practical questions.

What will my website cost?

We agree the scope and quote first. The number of pages, content, design and integrations shape the project. Hosting, ongoing marketing and automation are priced separately, so you know what the build includes.

Can you work with my existing website?

Yes. We review the current pages, useful content, search traffic and enquiry routes before deciding what to retain or rebuild. Existing URLs and redirects are part of the launch plan.

Can our team approve all of the copy?

Yes. Practice descriptions, biographies and contact wording pass through your review before publication.

Can we keep our client portal?

Yes. The public website can direct existing clients to the system your firm already uses.

Will each lawyer have a profile?

We can build a consistent biography format with individual experience, practice areas and contact routes.

Can you help with local search?

Yes. The scope can include practice pages, office information, technical SEO and Google Business Profile support.
