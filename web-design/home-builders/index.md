## A portfolio should tell the story behind the finish.

Strong photography earns attention; the accompanying story explains the value of your work. A project page can describe the site, the client’s priorities, the materials and decisions that shaped the home. That turns an image gallery into useful evidence for a future client.

I design the portfolio around the way people compare builders. Visitors can move between relevant homes, understand your approach and see what an initial conversation involves. The site should feel as considered as the work it presents.

A consistent project format makes future publishing easier. Photographs, a short film and a well-written case can support the website, social channels and presentations without starting again each time.
