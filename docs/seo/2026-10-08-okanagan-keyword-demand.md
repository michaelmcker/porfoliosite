# Okanagan keyword demand — October 8, 2026

Research only. No website or campaign changes.

Refreshed Google Ads historical volumes through DataForSEO for 273 phrases across 11 Okanagan city/municipality filters (3,003 observations), plus 7,298 Kelowna keyword suggestions. Returned history is September 2025–August 2026, English, Google search sites only. Live location catalogue verified. API cost US$1.08.

## Main findings

Kelowna-filtered average monthly searches: web design kelowna 170; seo kelowna 210; website design near me 70; marketing agency kelowna 70. Wix 880, WordPress 210 and Webflow 90 are broad brand terms; Wix website design and WordPress developer each show 10, Webflow developer 0. Do not equate brand volume with people hiring a designer.

Priorities: improve existing Web Design page; develop an evidence-led local SEO offer; then consider substantive WordPress and Wix service pages where delivery scope is actually offered. Webflow is a capability niche rather than the strongest local volume target. Use one cost resource and one platform comparison, not city/platform permutations. These are analyst priorities, not ranking or revenue forecasts.

Monthly history matters: web design kelowna averages 170 but June–August average is 130; website design near me averages 40 in Vernon but all three recent months report zero. Zero, unavailable and missing results remain distinct. Close variants are not summed. CPC is USD; Ads competition is not organic difficulty.
