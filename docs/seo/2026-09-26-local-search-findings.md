## Geographic targeting

Organic SEO cannot be geo-fenced. Google uses relevance, distance and prominence for local results. Service-area schema describes coverage; it does not create an office or override the searcher's distance. See [Google's local ranking guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091?hl=en).

For tighter coverage, complete or verify a Google Business Profile under the real trading identity with the actual service areas, accurate service categories, work photos and genuine reviews. Link it to the most relevant service page. Do not create a fictional Kelowna address. Repository records say the Search Console domain verification and dedicated portfolio analytics were unfinished; that account state has not been reverified in this release.

Exact city/radius audience targeting belongs in Google Ads and other paid campaigns. No ads were created and no advertising budget was spent on campaigns.
