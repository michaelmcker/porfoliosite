## Execution priorities

1. Use the existing web-design page for Kelowna and Vernon website design/development, with AI Catalyst, Okanagan Treehouse and RCCV proof. Avoid near-duplicate city doorway pages.
2. Keep marketing and branding focused on websites, social media, video and brand development. Support narrower video/social/local-SEO queries with relevant work rather than a generic list of locations.
3. Keep AI implementation grounded in custom skills, workflows, agents and training tied to bookings, revenue and time saved. Search data is sparse; useful proof and real customer language matter more than inflated keyword lists.
4. Confirm Search Console ownership and indexing, and establish a genuine Google Business Profile/service area. These account-side actions have not been verified in this revision. Organic pages cannot be geo-fenced; location relevance, local proof, links and a legitimate business profile support local discovery. Paid campaigns can use geographic targeting.
5. Measure actual impressions, queries, enquiries and bookings before expanding content. No rank or indexing improvement is claimed from this deployment.
