## Content contract
Each article has one commercial-information intent, a direct opening answer, useful distinct H2s, a short decision table or numbered list where appropriate, three specific FAQs, current named sources and original portfolio evidence. Comparison lists include Michael and explain suitability; do not invent rankings, firsthand tests, client rosters, prices or 2027 evidence. Each draft links contextually to one primary money page, a relevant case study and two useful sibling articles. Research each vertical separately before drafting its comparison.
