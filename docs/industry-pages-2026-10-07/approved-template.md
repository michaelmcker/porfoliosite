# Industry pages — rough visual template
7 October 2026 · Concept for review · Not implemented or published

[Open the visual review](index.html)

Four image-first concepts demonstrate a shared visual system using plumbers as the example. Generated with the built-in image tool from the approved web-service concept and the actual Cool Runnings website capture.

## Direction
Keep the portfolio's white canvas, DM Sans, Fraunces, forest green and gold. Lead with the commercial offer, show real work early, then explain what a useful website needs to do for this particular industry. Open editorial rows carry detail.

The images demonstrate representative sections, not the entire page or final copy. They do not replace the individually researched briefs.

## Full page rhythm
1. Offer, relevant work and one consultation action.
2. Attributed proof: a relevant result or a design case.
3. What this industry's website needs, with concrete examples.
4. A closer look at actual work: original screenshots with short explanations.
5. Discovery in practice: local service and area information, Google foundations and accurate content for AI search.
6. From interest to enquiry: the appropriate form, phone route, booking handoff, donation or consultation path.
7. Scope, delivery, ownership, platform choice, training and support.
8. Industry-specific questions and a consultation offer.

Sections can combine or change order. Use the detailed individual brief for content, not a repeated set of generic headings.

## Shared visual rules
- Use the shared production navigation and footer.
- DM Sans for body and outcome headings; Fraunces for editorial emphasis, project titles and metrics.
- White #FFFFFF, ink #171813, forest #243C31, gold #E3A916, paper #F7F7F3.
- Content width about 1280px; desktop gutters 48–80px; mobile gutters 22px.
- Body 18–20px, paragraphs around 45–65 characters wide.
- Section spacing 64–96px desktop, 40–56px mobile. No minimum viewport-height sections.
- Open layouts and hairline separators. Paper panels only for distinct supporting material.
- One finished-work visual at a time; frames fit the content.
- Real case attribution in the surrounding copy, no decorative illustrative labels.
- Mostly static content. Motion must not be necessary to understand the offer.

## Adaptation by industry
| Vertical | Central decision | Relevant proof | Content treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plumbers / trades | Can this site bring useful enquiries and the right jobs? | ABC intake; attributed Cool Runnings result | Job-type rows; service/coverage example |
| Hotels / accommodation | Why stay, and how do I book? | Treehouse design | Property-led gallery; booking journey |
| Charities / schools | Why trust and support this organisation? | St James design, marketing, donor film | Story, evidence, donation/enquiry route |
| Professional services | Is this the right firm for my situation? | Closest applicable genuine work | Expertise, fit, consultation expectations |

The shared design does not mean identical headlines, section order, examples, depth or FAQs.
