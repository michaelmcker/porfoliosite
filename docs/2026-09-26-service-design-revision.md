# Service-page design revision

The three pages remain part of the Michael McKerracher portfolio. The homepage's DM Sans, Fraunces, white canvas, paper surfaces, hard gold, black device edge and restrained motion are the design foundation.

The reference review yielded four useful moves:

- [The Matter of Design](https://www.thematterofdesign.com/) gives each idea a distinct visual field and lets the work occupy a proportionate space. The service pages use generous spacing and content-sized media, without adopting its multicolour grid.
- [Marimba](https://marimba.design/) treats the headline and process as core design elements. The service pages retain the portfolio's fonts and make the engagement process easy to scan.
- [Pleurat](https://www.pleurat.com/), linked from the supplied [Awwwards entry](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/product-designer-portfolio-5), leads with the work and describes the practice plainly. We use sourced projects rather than a dashboard of invented interactions.
- [Mulberry](https://lexingtonthemes.com/viewports/mulberry) uses clear service copy, proof and consultation flow. The service pages apply that clarity to Michael's work and custom pricing.

AI implementation leads with a static illustrative booking calendar. Selected workflow artwork remains sourced from the approved portfolio artifacts and links to deeper explanations. Web design keeps the AI Catalyst website in the hero as a non-interactive specimen, then shows the RCCV laptop still and current Okanagan Treehouse project imagery. Marketing and branding retains the St. James film and shows Vertical Impression and Cool Runnings with correctly fitted screenshots.

No portfolio-homepage redesign is necessary for this revision. Its interactive work remains where a visitor can explore it; the commercial pages prioritize legibility and the next contact action.

The local page could not be inspected in the browser because loopback browser access was blocked. Asset references, generated HTML/Markdown parity and repository tests must pass before reporting the revision as ready for visual review.
