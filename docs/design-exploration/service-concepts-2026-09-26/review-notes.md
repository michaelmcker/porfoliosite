# Nine service-page visual concepts

Three independent page architectures for each offering. Created with the built-in image generation tool. These are visual proposals, not coded service pages or conversion-tested results.

## Directions
- 01: Work-led agency — split opening, alternating proof, forest chapter, gold close.
- 02: Outcome-led SaaS — centered opening, dominant visual, benefit chapters, clear offer and FAQ.
- 03: Editorial studio — large headline, cinematic strip, asymmetric proof and quieter process.

## Review observations
- Web 01 and Marketing 01 most closely translate the homepage's dimensional work objects and white canvas.
- Web 02 gives AI Catalyst the strongest central stage. Its generated RCCV screen has a mistaken Catalyst label; use the original RCCV asset in any build.
- Marketing 03 has the most distinctive opening and image rhythm. Preserve original film frames and identity lettering in implementation.
- AI 02 most directly connects a customer enquiry to a booking. All AI lifestyle scenes are illustrative, not client photographs or results.
- The AI round is too serif-heavy relative to the current homepage; implementation must use the actual DM Sans/Fraunces font files and heading hierarchy.
- Generated claims such as 'proven process' or results 'within weeks' are not verified copy and must be removed. Numbers of parishes and project descriptions also require source-correct wording.
- Some tiny labels and dates are image-generation artifacts. These compositions establish direction; they do not establish final copy, accessibility, responsiveness, SEO implementation or exact source fidelity.
- Rejected initial web generations invented unrelated Cool Runnings businesses. Those were replaced and are not included among the nine selected files.
