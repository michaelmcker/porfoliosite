The strategy and the things
you need to put it to work.

Engagements are built around the commercial problem, whether that is clarifying an offer, launching something new or improving the material your team uses to sell.

Positioning & messaging

Audience, value proposition, offer structure and the language that explains why your business is a good fit. A practical foundation for your website and sales conversations.

Brand direction

Visual direction, typography, imagery and tone that work together. We define what needs to be created or refined, then apply it to the agreed materials.

Campaigns & content

Campaign ideas, landing pages, creative and supporting content, connected to a clear action such as an enquiry, booking or sales conversation.

Sales materials

Presentations, proposals and product stories that make the offer tangible. Where it makes sense, we can also build a repeatable way to tailor that material.

Marketing support for
being found and chosen.

I work with Okanagan businesses on the parts of marketing that connect discovery to a sale. We can focus on a specific launch, improve your local presence or build a coordinated plan across your website, content and sales materials.

Local SEO & service content

Review how customers search for your services in Vernon, Kelowna and the communities you serve. Plan useful service pages, improve internal links and review your Google Business Profile information. Write content around genuine customer questions and the work you can demonstrate.

Brand strategy & messaging

Clarify your audience, offer, value proposition and reasons to believe. Turn that into a messaging guide your team can use in website copy, campaigns, proposals and customer conversations.

Visual identity & brand application

Establish the typography, colours, imagery and layout direction. Scope the identity work and apply it consistently across the website, presentations and campaign materials people actually see.

Campaign planning & creative

Build a campaign around a specific offer and action. Develop the message, landing page, creative and supporting email or social content, with a production plan and defined review steps. Media spend and channel management are scoped separately.

Content & product marketing

Explain services and products through demonstrations, customer questions, case studies and useful articles. Create a realistic publishing plan with source material and clear ownership, so the content stays accurate.

Measurement & improvement

Agree which calls, enquiries or sales actions matter. Review the available website and campaign data, distinguish traffic from qualified demand and use the findings to choose the next improvement.
