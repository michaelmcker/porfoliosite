A few things you might be wondering.

What should I prepare?

A list of the tools you use, examples of recurring work and the questions you want answered. We agree what is needed beforehand so you can avoid sharing unnecessary sensitive information.

Does the audit include building the automations?

No. It covers assessment and a detailed, actionable report. Custom implementation and ongoing maintenance are a separate engagement.

Will the report guarantee savings?

No. It can estimate opportunities using information from your business, with assumptions clearly identified. Actual results need to be measured after an improvement is implemented and used.

Can the assessment cover every part of the company?

We agree the areas and workflows up front to keep the engagement useful within the available time. A broader or more complex assessment can be scoped separately.
