A few practical
questions.

What will my website cost?

We agree the scope and quote first. The number of pages, content, design and integrations shape the project. Hosting, ongoing marketing and automation are priced separately, so you know what the build includes.

Can you work with my existing website?

Yes. We review the current pages, useful content, search traffic and enquiry routes before deciding what to retain or rebuild. Existing URLs and redirects are part of the launch plan.

Can we keep our booking system?

Yes. We connect the website to the appointment tools your practice already uses.

Can reception update hours and notices?

Yes. We can provide straightforward editing for the information that changes most often.

Is this only useful for attracting new patients?

The site can also help existing patients and reduce repeated administrative questions. The priorities follow your practice’s needs.

Who reviews the medical service descriptions?

Your clinical team reviews and approves those descriptions before publication.
