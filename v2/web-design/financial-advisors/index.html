A few practical
questions.

What will my website cost?

We agree the scope and quote first. The number of pages, content, design and integrations shape the project. Hosting, ongoing marketing and automation are priced separately, so you know what the build includes.

Can you work with my existing website?

Yes. We review the current pages, useful content, search traffic and enquiry routes before deciding what to retain or rebuild. Existing URLs and redirects are part of the launch plan.

Can the website follow our firm’s approval process?

Yes. We build copy review and required disclosures into the project plan.

Can we use our existing meeting scheduler?

Yes. We can connect it to the relevant pages and make the booking handoff clear.

Can you create content for different client groups?

Yes. Each page can explain the actual services and relationship relevant to that group.

Can you help with photography and video?

Yes. Original team photography and a short introductory film can be planned alongside the website.
