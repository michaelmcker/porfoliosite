A few practical
questions.

What will my website cost?

We agree the scope and quote first. The number of pages, content, design and integrations shape the project. Hosting, ongoing marketing and automation are priced separately, so you know what the build includes.

Can you work with my existing website?

Yes. We review the current pages, useful content, search traffic and enquiry routes before deciding what to retain or rebuild. Existing URLs and redirects are part of the launch plan.

Can you help us attract larger landscaping projects?

Yes. We can lead with the project types, visual quality and process that speak to those buyers, while giving maintenance enquiries their own route.

Can we use the photos we already have?

Yes. We review your existing material, select what tells the story well and identify any useful gaps before commissioning more.

Can the website cover several Okanagan communities?

Yes. We organise the real coverage and add local pages where you have useful service information or project evidence.

Do you offer ongoing SEO and marketing?

Yes. Search content, Google Business Profile support, campaigns, video and follow-up workflows can be scoped around your priorities.
