How to turn this list into a useful shortlist

A Kelowna web-design shortlist should start with the business problem and the kind of work required. Choose providers whose demonstrated services match that brief, then ask each to respond to the same scope.

  • Name the priority services and the customer action the site needs to support.
  • Identify the content and images you already have.
  • List booking, payment or CRM requirements.
  • Ask for account ownership, training and support terms.
  • Request a clear split between the build and ongoing marketing.

A considered proposal should explain the approach and assumptions. The website cost checklist helps compare what is included. The Kelowna website guide helps turn the business objective into a brief.

Questions, answered.

Is a freelancer or an agency better for a Kelowna business website?

Choose by scope and working style. A hands-on independent designer can suit a focused website and close collaboration. An agency can suit a project needing multiple specialists or ongoing team capacity. Compare relevant work, ownership and support rather than assuming size determines quality.

Are all these designers physically based in Kelowna?

No. This is a shortlist for Kelowna businesses that includes providers elsewhere in the Okanagan. Michael McKerracher is based in Coldstream. Check the provider’s actual location and meeting arrangements when local access matters.

Who should I choose if I also need branding, video or automation?

Include those requirements in the brief before requesting quotes. Michael McKerracher offers website design, marketing and branding, video work and AI implementation. Ask any shortlisted provider which services are delivered directly and which require a separate partner.
