AI implementation,
from enquiry to follow-up.

The engagement covers the working system: the information it needs, the tools it connects, the output your team uses and the checks that keep it useful.

Understand where business gets stuck.

We look at how people find you, what happens after an enquiry, how your team prepares the work and where follow-up slows down. We choose a result to measure: qualified enquiries, calls, bookings, response time or hours spent on a recurring task.

Build the missing connection.

I configure suitable tools, connect the systems you already use and build the custom parts where needed. That could be an enquiry-to-proposal workflow, a content and publishing process or a reporting system that makes the next action easier to see.

Put it into the team’s working day.

We test on representative work, decide what needs approval and train the people who will use it. Instructions, permissions, failure handling and handover are part of making the system usable.

Measure it and keep improving.

Once it is in use, we review the result against the starting point. Ongoing work covers maintenance and the next agreed improvements, so the system can keep up with changes in your business.

Do I need to know which AI tools to use?

No. Bring the business problem. Part of the work is deciding whether an existing tool, a straightforward automation or a custom system is the right fit.

Can I use the audit without hiring you to build?

Yes. The report is a standalone deliverable. It includes actionable recommendations and off-the-shelf options you can implement yourself.

How do we know if it is working?

We agree a starting point and a measure that fits the project. More qualified enquiries, faster responses, more completed follow-ups and reduced preparation time need different measurements. We review what actually changes.
