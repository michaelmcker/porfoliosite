## What should a Vernon owner ask before choosing?

A Vernon website proposal should make the work understandable to the owner who will use and maintain it. Bring a short brief describing the services, communities covered and the way customers book or enquire.

For a trade, ask how the site will distinguish the jobs you want from requests you cannot serve. For a practice, ask how a new client finds the right service and prepares for an appointment. For hospitality, ask how imagery, availability and booking details will connect.

- Who writes and approves the service content?
- Which account logins will the business control?
- How are enquiries delivered and tested?
- What training is included?
- What are the ongoing costs and cancellation terms?

## Questions, answered.

### Do I need a designer with an office in Vernon?

Not necessarily. Relevant work, clear communication and reliable support are important. If in-person meetings matter, confirm them directly. Michael McKerracher is based in nearby Coldstream and serves Vernon and the wider Okanagan.

### Which option suits an owner who cannot write all the website copy?

Choose a proposal that explicitly includes interviews, page planning and writing. Michael McKerracher can include that work in the scope. For any package, check whether supplying text is your responsibility before comparing the price.

### Can I start with a small site and add North Okanagan pages later?

Yes. Agree a structure and editing approach that can grow. Launch useful core pages first, then add local pages when you have distinct coverage details, relevant work or customer questions to answer.
