# The Kelowna business website guide: 9 decisions for 2026

A Kelowna business website needs clear service pages, real proof, accurate local coverage and an easy way to call, book or request a quote. Plan the customer journey and content before choosing a platform; build mobile layouts, search-ready headings and links, reliable form delivery and ownership into the scope. Measure qualified enquiries and booked work after launch, then improve the pages where suitable customers drop out.

## 1. Decide which customer action matters

A Kelowna business website works best when it is built around a useful customer action. That might be a qualified quote request, a first appointment, a reservation or a purchase. These actions need different information. A restaurant guest wants the menu and availability. A homeowner considering a renovation wants relevant work and confidence in the builder.

Write down your three most valuable services, the customers you want more of and the enquiries you cannot serve. Give that brief to your designer. “More leads” is much easier to work on when it really means more kitchen renovation consultations from homeowners inside your service area.

## 2. Separate residents, visitors and professional buyers

Match the website to the decision each customer is trying to make. Kelowna businesses can serve very different audiences from the same site. [Tourism Kelowna](https://www.tourismkelowna.com/)
 presents stays, food and drink, events and experiences as distinct planning needs. A visitor organising a trip needs dates and practical details that a returning local customer may already know.

Match the website to the decision

| Customer | What helps them choose | Useful next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Homeowner needing a repair | Service, coverage, availability and relevant reviews | Call or request service |
| Visitor planning a stay | Photos, location, dates and booking policies | Check availability |
| Client choosing a professional | Experience, scope, approach and what an appointment involves | Request a consultation |

Use the homepage to send people to the detail they need. One generic contact form can leave every visitor in the same pile, with the owner doing the sorting later.

## 3. Plan useful pages before choosing a theme

A practical Kelowna service website usually needs a homepage, individual service pages, work or results, an about page and a clear contact route. A service page should explain who the service is for, the problems covered, the process, the evidence and what happens after an enquiry.

An [appliance repair website](https://michaelmck.site/web-design/appliance-repair/)
, for example, can separate refrigerator, dishwasher and laundry repairs because each service raises different fault, access and booking questions. [ABC Appliance](https://www.abcappliance.ca/)
 is relevant work from my portfolio: the design and service content support a direct request for help. Useful detail can earn its own page. A second heading by itself is not enough.

If customers ask genuinely different local questions, add a service-area page with real coverage information and relevant work. Avoid publishing a page for every neighbourhood before you have something useful to say about it.

## 4. Compare the scope behind the price

A website budget only becomes meaningful when the quote names the work behind it. At a planning budget of CAD $3,000 to $4,000, prioritise a focused service website with a considered design, useful core content, mobile contact actions and the search foundations. A large catalogue, complex booking system or ongoing campaign needs its own scope.

Ask whether copywriting, photography, migration, redirects, training and ongoing support are included. A cheaper build that leaves most of the content to you may cost more of your time. My [website projects](https://michaelmck.site/web-design/)
 are custom-quoted, and the budget discussion starts with what the site needs to accomplish. Use the Okanagan website cost checklist to compare proposals consistently.

## 5. Show evidence where customers hesitate

Website proof should answer the question already in the visitor’s mind. Put repair experience beside the repair service, a relevant project beside the renovation offer and room details beside an accommodation booking action. A logo strip cannot do all of that work by itself.

My projects include ABC Appliance, the Okanagan Treehouse design and Cool Runnings’ website, local SEO and conversion work. [Cool Runnings](https://michaelmck.site/v2/work/local-search-magnet.html)
 saw a 30% increase in qualified bookings. Each example supports a different conversation: making a service clear, presenting a distinctive place or turning local interest into enquiries. Choose the evidence that fits the decision your own customer needs to make.

## 6. Connect the website to local search

A Kelowna website and its Google Business Profile need to describe the same business. Keep services, location or service area, contact details and hours accurate. Google says local results mainly depend on relevance, distance and prominence, so a page title cannot change where a searcher is standing.

Give each important page a descriptive title, a clear main heading, readable service information and useful internal links. Ask the designer to handle crawlability, canonical URLs and the sitemap. Then connect Search Console so you can see whether Google has found the pages and which searches create impressions. The Google and ChatGPT visibility checklist explains the related AI-search work.

## 7. Walk through the enquiry on a phone

A mobile website review should include completing the customer’s task, not just looking at a small screenshot. Find a service, check coverage, tap the phone number, submit the form and read the confirmation. Make sure the owner receives the enquiry.

Use real labels on fields and plain explanations for errors. Ask only for the information needed to take the next step. An initial quote form might need a name, contact details, location and a short description, while project photos can be optional. Requiring a full account or an exact project budget too early can make the enquiry unnecessarily difficult.

## 8. Agree who owns and updates the website

Kelowna business owners should keep access to the domain, hosting or platform account, analytics and business profile. Record who can edit the pages, who receives form notifications and who is responsible for updates.

A simple website can be inexpensive to operate. A site with frequent offers, booking rules or staff changes may need more editing control and training. Ask for a handover that includes account access, editing instructions, renewal costs and a way to export or move the content. Decide these details before paying for the build.

## 9. Measure the work you want more of

A website launch should establish a starting point for qualified enquiries and booked work. Count completed form submissions separately from phone-button taps. A tap can show intent, but it does not establish that a conversation or sale occurred.

Review the source of enquiries, the service requested and the reason unsuitable enquiries were declined. If traffic grows but the jobs are wrong, revisit the service language and coverage. If suitable people enquire but do not book, examine response time and follow-up. A website becomes more useful when those business observations inform the next change.

## Questions, answered.

### Can I launch my Kelowna website before every service page is ready?

Yes. Launch the complete pages people need to understand the business and make an enquiry, prioritising your most important services. Test the phone, form and confirmation journey on mobile before launch. Add further pages when their content is ready, with an owner assigned to the remaining work. Keep empty pages out of the navigation.

### Do I need a separate website for a second Kelowna location?

Usually one website can support multiple genuine locations. Give each location its own accurate address, hours, services and contact route, including useful differences in staff or visiting arrangements. Separate websites may suit distinct brands or operating models, but another address does not automatically require another domain. Choose the structure your team can keep accurate and customers can navigate easily.

### Can a designer help write the website content as well?

Yes. Michael McKerracher offers content planning and writing alongside website design. Ask for discovery, service copy, project stories, FAQs, migration and photography to be defined in the quote. Supply service facts, actual work and recurring customer questions, and assign someone in the business to verify accuracy. Agree who gives final approval so finished pages do not stall between the designer and the team.

### What should be in a Kelowna website launch checklist?

Check every important service page on a phone, test enquiry delivery and confirm account ownership. Review redirects from the old website, indexing controls, canonical URLs, sitemap and analytics access. Keep a record of the live URLs and the starting point for qualified enquiries so later work has something meaningful to compare against.

### How should my website connect with my Google Business Profile?

Use consistent service, location, hours and contact information, with links to the relevant website destination. Keep the profile accurate and maintain the actual business location. Review website enquiries alongside available profile performance rather than assuming every Google visit came from the map listing.
