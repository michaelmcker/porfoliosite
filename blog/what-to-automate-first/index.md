# What should an Okanagan business automate first?

A useful first AI project usually starts with a task your team repeats: preparing an estimate, assembling a proposal, following up an enquiry or producing a weekly report. You should be able to show an example of a good result and name the person who checks it. If nobody agrees what the output should look like, clarify the process before automating it.

## Follow one enquiry through the business.

Take a recent customer enquiry and trace what happened. Where did it arrive? Who picked it up? What information was missing? How was an estimate prepared, and who followed up? Write down the waiting time as well as the working time. A five-minute task that sits untouched for two days may matter more than a long task that already runs well.

## Compare frequency, effort and consequence.

Make a short list of repeated tasks. For each, record how often it happens, the approximate time it takes, the inputs it needs and the consequence of a wrong result. Proposal preparation may be a good candidate because a person can review the draft. Automatic pricing or customer commitments need tighter rules and more scrutiny. Start with a task where the team can catch mistakes before they cause problems.

## Use an existing tool when it covers the job.

ChatGPT Projects can organize files and instructions around recurring work. Gemini Gems can provide tailored instructions and reference material. Claude Cowork can support tasks involving files and connected tools. Those are possible starting points, not a reason to force every process into the same platform. Check your plan, permissions and actual task before selecting a tool.

## Build the connections that are missing.

A custom workflow becomes useful when work needs to pass reliably between systems. For example, an enquiry form might create a customer record, assemble a draft proposal and put it into a review queue. Decide what happens when a field is missing or a connection fails. Someone should be able to see the problem and complete the work manually.

## Measure the first version before expanding.

Record the starting point and review a representative set of completed work. Compare preparation time, corrections and completed follow-ups. Bookings and revenue can matter too, but changes in demand, pricing and seasonality affect those numbers. Keep the measure close enough to the workflow that you can understand what improved.
