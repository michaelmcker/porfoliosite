## How to turn this list into a useful shortlist

A Kelowna web-design shortlist should start with the business problem and the kind of work required. Choose providers whose demonstrated services match that brief, then ask each to respond to the same scope.

- Name the priority services and the customer action the site needs to support.
- Identify the content and images you already have.
- List booking, payment or CRM requirements.
- Ask for account ownership, training and support terms.
- Request a clear split between the build and ongoing marketing.

## Questions, answered.

### Is a freelancer or an agency better for a Kelowna business website?

Choose by scope and working style. A hands-on independent designer can suit a focused website and close collaboration; an agency can suit work needing several specialists or continuing team capacity. Send the same brief to each provider. Ask who will do the work, how approvals happen, what you will own and what support follows launch. Compare a relevant project and an editing demonstration rather than assuming team size determines quality.

### Are all these designers physically based in Kelowna?

No. This shortlist serves Kelowna buyers and includes providers elsewhere in the Okanagan. Michael McKerracher is based in Coldstream. Keep a provider’s office location separate from the areas it serves. If face-to-face access matters, confirm where meetings happen and how often. Compare relevant work, availability and ongoing support alongside location.

### Who should I choose if I also need branding, video or automation?

Include each requirement in the brief before requesting quotes. Michael McKerracher offers website design, branding, video, marketing and AI implementation. Ask each provider for deliverables and relevant examples for the services you need. Confirm what is delivered directly, what involves partners and who coordinates the handoffs. A combined proposal should still make the individual scopes and ongoing responsibilities clear.

### What should I ask a Kelowna web designer before signing?

Ask for a relevant project, the proposed page structure, who writes the copy and how enquiries will be tested. Confirm platform and account ownership, redirects, analytics access and ongoing support. Request a clear list of exclusions and milestones so the proposal describes the work rather than relying on a polished portfolio alone.
