## Put the scope, ownership and launch checks in writing

A West Kelowna website quote should identify who creates the content, which integrations are included and who maintains the finished site. The location of the provider is only one part of the working relationship.

Use the same brief for each shortlisted firm: desired customer action, essential pages, available images, budget range, booking tools and update needs. Then ask how the provider will test the mobile experience and hand over account access.

## Questions, answered.

### Does the designer need a West Kelowna office?

No. This shortlist includes Okanagan providers for West Kelowna businesses, not a claim that every firm has a West Kelowna office. Confirm availability, communication and meeting arrangements alongside portfolio relevance.

### What should I ask a designer about a winery or accommodation site?

Ask how imagery, practical visitor information, availability and booking conditions will work together. Review the complete mobile route into the booking platform, not just the homepage design.

### Can one provider handle the website and ongoing marketing?

Yes, when those services are included in the agreed scope. Michael McKerracher offers website, marketing and branding work. Compare the build fee, ongoing work, reporting and ownership terms separately so the arrangement is clear.
